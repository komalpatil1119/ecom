Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PrintYouth T-shirt design bundles before you buy.

What file formats are included in each T-shirt design bundle?

Every T-shirt design bundle from PrintYouth includes PNG (transparent background, 300 DPI), PSD (layered Photoshop file), and Adobe Illustrator AI files. All formats are print-ready and compatible with every major POD platform.

Can I sell on multiple POD platforms with one license?

Yes. The commercial license included with every bundle covers unlimited use across all POD platforms — Printful, Printify, Merch by Amazon, Etsy, Redbubble, Teepublic, and more. No royalties, no attribution required.

Is there a limit on how many products I can sell?

No. The commercial license has no sales cap. You can sell unlimited copies of products using PrintYouth designs across any number of stores and platforms.

How do I download my designs after purchase?

You receive an instant download link by email immediately after payment. You can also access all your files from the order confirmation page.

Are PrintYouth designs compatible with Printful and Printify?

Yes. All designs are delivered as 300 DPI PNG files with transparent backgrounds — the exact format required by Printful, Printify, and most DTG printing platforms.
